Cosmic Rules & Pantheon Guide

Welcome, Traveler, to Yahtzee: Pantheon of the Cosmos.

  • Split Scorecard: Upper Deities (Ones through Sixes) on the left, Lower Constellations on the right.
  • Chance Cards: Drawn at the start of every round. They grant divine blessings (+5 to +15 pts), cosmic drag (-5 to -15 pts), Supernova (instant Yahtzee), or Black Hole (set a category to zero).
  • Objective: Align all 13 celestial constellations to achieve the highest possible Pantheon score!
